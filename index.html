Our Story

A Jeweler Worth Knowing
for Four Decades

Rebecca's Fine Jewelry has served the Birmingham and McCalla community since 1984. We carry fine jewelry, design custom pieces, and handle repairs — with the same personal attention we've always offered.

Our customers come in for engagement rings, wedding bands, heirloom repairs, meaningful gifts, and pieces that mark the moments that matter. They keep coming back because we take the time to get it right.

Family-owned and community-rooted for over 40 years. That means something to us — and we think it shows.

The Rebeccas & Team

Rebecca's Fine Jewelry is more than a family-owned business — it is a family legacy, now spanning three generations and four decades in the Birmingham community.

The Rebeccas — photo coming soon

Rebecca Middlebrooks Grandmother & Co-Founder
Rebecca Lynn Founder & Master Jeweler
Rebecca Ellie Next Generation

Rebecca's Fine Jewelry was founded 40 years ago by Rebecca Lynn, our master jeweler and the creative force behind everything we do. She built this store from the ground up — her craftsmanship, her standards, and her commitment to every customer are the reason people have trusted us for decades.

Behind the scenes, Rebecca Middlebrooks — Rebecca Lynn's mother and our grandmother — was a constant presence in the business, quietly keeping things running with grace and dedication until she retired at 88. Her work ethic and warmth shaped the culture of this store in ways that are felt every day.

Now, Rebecca Ellie is stepping into the family business — bringing the next generation into a story that started long before her and will continue long after. Three Rebeccas. One store. Forty years and counting.
